How to survive

Draft: tuck within a couple of metres of the wheel ahead — 35% less drag, so you hold pack speed for far less effort and your legs come back.

Banking: the top is a longer lap, but it is stored height. Climb on the back straight, then dive through the last turn — the drop is free speed exactly where you need it.

Legs: empty them and you soft‑pedal until they come back. That is how the devil gets you.
