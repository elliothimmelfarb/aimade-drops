POLYNYA

Night on the pack. You have an icebreaker, a convoy of thin-hulled freighters, and a port at the north edge of the field. Everything between is ice.

Controls
W / ↑ telegraph up (toward FULL AHEAD)
S / ↓ telegraph down (through STOP into ASTERN)
A D / ← → rudder port & starboard
Space / H horn — convoy HOLD / FOLLOW
P / Esc pause  ·  M mute  ·  R restart
Touch telegraph left, rudder right, horn centre
How it plays
Thick ice stops you dead. Back astern down your own channel and ram it again — the cracks you left are still there.
The channel refreezes behind you. Freighters caught in it go beset; break the ice around them to cut them loose.
The horn holds the convoy. Park them in open water while you pre-break a ridge, then call them on.
