SURPLACE

Match sprint on the boards. Two riders, two laps, first across the line.

The rider in front does all the work. Sit on your opponent's wheel and the draft saves your strength, so neither of you wants to lead, and the race can slow to a dead stop while you both wait. Your legs hold about twenty seconds of full sprint. Spend them once, at the right moment.

Track bikes have no brakes. Ease off to slow, or climb the banking and trade speed for height. Dive back down and the slope returns it.

  • Pedal hold Space, mouse button, or the right side of the screen
  • Banking ↑ ↓ or W S, mouse height, or drag the left side
  • Balance at a standstill ← →, mouse left and right, or slide on the left side
  • Keys P pause · M mute · R restart

After the 200 metre board, hold your line: swerving into the other rider forfeits the heat. If the race stands still too long, the referee orders it on.

Drafting inside six metres saves strength. Climbing the banking trades speed for height and a dive gives it back. After the 200 board, hold your line. Best of three heats wins the match.
