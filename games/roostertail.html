ROOSTERTAIL

UNLIMITED HEAT · 3 LAPS · FLYING START

Three tonnes of turbine hydroplane, and the hull is a wing. Wind her up and aerodynamic lift unloads her until only the sponson tips and half the propeller touch water — that near-flying state is the fast one, and it is also the one that kills. The wheel sits below the centre of gravity, so throttle is pitch: feed her power and the nose comes up. Too far, too fast, and the air gets under her and she goes over backwards. SPACE drops the canard and pushes the nose back down, at a price in speed. Time your run so you cross the line flat out as the clock hits zero.
