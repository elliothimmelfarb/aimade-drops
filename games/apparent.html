APPARENT

Outrun the wind · DN iceboat time trial

Three steel runners on black ice carry almost no friction, so an iceboat does something a sailboat cannot: it manufactures its own wind. Every metre per second you gain swings the breeze you feel further forward and makes it stronger, which makes you faster, which swings it further still. Three laps, up to the windward mark and back through the gate. The clock is the only opponent.

THE ONE THING TO KNOW: straight downwind is the slow way. Sail 40° off it and zig-zag — you will get there in half the time.
