PLENUM

AMPHIBIOUS CIRCUIT · 3 LAPS · 5 CRAFT

A Formula-class racing hovercraft. Three laps of a real amphibious circuit — grass, a packed-dirt esse section, a mud bog, and two water crossings. You ride on a cushion of air, so you do not grip the ground: you are a puck with a propeller.

TWO THINGS THAT WILL DECIDE YOUR RACE
1
No brakes — steer with thrust. The rudder vanes sit in the propeller wash. Off the throttle the nose barely answers. Keep the power on and control your speed with your line.
2
Hit the water fast. Below hump speed you are climbing your own bow wave and the drag is enormous. Arrive flying and you skim across; arrive slow and you will wallow while the field skates past.
