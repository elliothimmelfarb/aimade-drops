Estafette

A battle commanded by mounted courier.

Hold the bridge and the ford until nightfall. You watch from the hill in the west; the attack comes over the river.

You never steer your brigades. Select one, point at the map, and an aide-de-camp gallops out with the order. Nothing changes until the rider arrives, and the battle moves while he rides. You have four aides. Riders can be shot.

Brigades fight on their own: fire, flanks and morale. A brigade struck through its flank breaks fast, yours and theirs alike.

Brigades face the way they marched and wheel slowly. Riders take seconds to arrive; the line under the crosshair shows the ride time. Guns limber before moving and unlimber before firing. Charges belong on flanks and shaken troops; a formed front throws cavalry back.
