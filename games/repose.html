REPOSE

You are an antlion larva. Dig a pit in live sand and eat what slides in.

Flick sand to dig. Fresh sand is loose and carries insects down to your jaws. Sand that sits sets firm and holds their weight, so keep your walls live. Every flick costs energy, every catch restores it.

When the bird's shadow crosses, hold still.

  • Click / tap flick sand at that point
  • Click in jaw circle snap
  • ← → or A D crawl
  • ↑ ↓ then X aim and flick (keyboard)
  • Space snap
  • P pause · M mute · R restart
