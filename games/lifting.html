The Amateur Athletic Association · Summer Meeting, 1908

LIFTING

Heel & Toe · 440 Yards · Heat, Semi-Final, Final

Two laws of the walk. One foot must always seem to touch the earth, and the advancing knee must be straight until it passes beneath you.

The trick: a judge cannot card what his eye cannot catch. Flight under 50 ms is invisible — and only three judges watch, each over one stretch of track. Ride the edge in sight; run riot in the blind.

The laws. Contact: never visibly leave the ground — flight under 50 ms is beneath the eye and goes unpunished. Straight knee: the advancing leg must stay straight from heel-strike until it passes under you. Each judge watches one stretch only: first offence he sees is a caution, second is a red card. Three red cards and you are escorted off.
