GIMBAL

You are the cook in a ship's galley, mid-storm. The whole galley rolls with the swell — and anything you are not holding slides. Pots skate, plates creep to the edge, dinner ends up on the deck. Cook the crew's orders anyway. Three failed orders and you're put ashore at the next port.

Keyboard

  • ADor arrows — walk
  • SPACEor E — interact: pick up, chop, flip, pour, serve, douse
  • Sor ↓ — hold to BRACE (grab the counter, ignore the stagger)
  • PMpause / mute  ·  R restart

Mouse & touch

  • TAPa spot to walk there
  • TAPwhere you already stand to interact
  • ACTbutton, bottom-left — interact
  • BRACEbutton, bottom-right — hold

The galley

  • PANTRY — biscuit, veg, fish
  • BOARD — chop (mash or hold)
  • STOVE — two burners
  • PLATING — pour & plate up
  • HATCH — serve the crew
  • BUCKET — on the bulkhead, for fires
Fiddle rails hold your pots — until the sea gets steeper than they are.
