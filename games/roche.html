PAUSED · CONTROLS

Both commanders order at once. Each round you commit exactly one order — MOVE or FIRE — and the round then plays back as a single deterministic simulation you both watch. A shell aimed at where someone was can miss them mid-dodge.

Your shell must climb out of your own gravity well, coast through the null band where the two pulls cancel, and fall up into the enemy's. Under-power it and it falls back on your head. The band is where the ion wind lives — near the surfaces the wind is nothing.

BINARY SYSTEM · L1 ENGAGEMENT

ROCHE

SIMULTANEOUS-TURN ARTILLERY ACROSS A GRAVITY NULL

Two worlds hang facing each other, close enough to share a sky. You stand on yours; the enemy hangs overhead on theirs. Up for you is down for them. Every shell has to climb out of your well, coast through the Roche null band where the two pulls cancel, and then fall upward into their well. Under-power a shot and it comes back down on your own head.

THE BAND IS THE GAMEBetween the lobes, gravity goes to nothing and the ion wind takes over. A slow shell can hang there for seconds and be blown somewhere else entirely.
ORDERS ARE SIMULTANEOUSMOVE or FIRE, committed blind, resolved together. Shoot where they will be, not where they are.
UNEVEN MASSESSome maps pair a heavy world with a light one. The null line sits off-centre, and the heavy side pays more to escape.
