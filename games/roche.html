PAUSED · CONTROLS

Both commanders order at once. Each round you commit exactly one order — MOVE or FIRE — and the round plays back as a single deterministic simulation you both watch. A shell aimed at where someone was can miss them mid-dodge.

Gravity is real and it comes from both worlds at once, falling off with the square of the distance. Shells can enter orbit, loop a world two or three times, or swing around the far side of the enemy planet and come down on their back. Under-power a shot and it returns to you — sometimes half an orbit later. Between the wells sits L1, where the pulls cancel and the ion wind takes over.

BINARY SYSTEM · L1 ENGAGEMENT

ROCHE

SIMULTANEOUS-TURN ORBITAL ARTILLERY

Two worlds, thousands of kilometres apart, and one artillery piece on each. Gravity here is the real thing — inverse-square, from both bodies at once — so a shell does not arc, it orbits. Loop your own world twice to build the geometry. Thread the L1 saddle where the pulls cancel. Swing around the far limb of the enemy planet and come down on their back. Under-power it and it returns to you, half an orbit later, on the wrong side of your own horizon.

SHOTS THAT ORBITEscape velocity sits around 70% power. Below that you are in orbit, not in flight — and everything you fire draws its ellipse across the sky for the next round to read.
ORDERS ARE SIMULTANEOUSMOVE or FIRE, committed blind, resolved together. Shoot where they will be, not where they are.
THE WORLDS TURNSome pairs spin. Your firing geometry is different every round, and camping a crater is not a plan.
