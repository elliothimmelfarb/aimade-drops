KEYLOG

Spring drive, 1890s. The timber has jammed at the narrows and the water is rising behind it. One log carries the whole pile. Sound the jam, find that log, pull it, and be off the timber when it moves. The mill pays by the log.

Move arrow keys, WASD, or tap a log
Sound a log step on it, or X for one in reach
Pry hold SPACE or press and hold on a log
Dynamite Q, then a log. Two sticks per drive
Balance a ride LEFT and RIGHT, or tap either side
Pause P or ESC   Restart R

A loose log thuds. A packed log knocks. A bound log rings high and will not move by hand. Pull off the logs pressing on it first.

PAUSED

Move arrow keys, WASD, or tap a log

Restart R

The ring tells the load. Thud is loose, knock is packed, a high ring is bound solid. Easing the logs above a bound log lightens it. When the key log goes, everything on it goes too.
