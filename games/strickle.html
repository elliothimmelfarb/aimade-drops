STRICKLEA BELL-FOUNDING DUEL

Stoking the furnace…

STRICKLETWO FOUNDERS · ONE RACK OF STAMPS

You and a rival are casting the ring for the same cathedral tower, working from one shared rack of brass letter punches. Stamp a name into a mold. Pour it and it becomes a bell — banked, safe, worth its length squared. Leave it sitting and your rival can break it open and recast it longer under a new name.

An unpoured mold is mud. At the final tally it is worth nothing at all. Every turn you leave a big word standing is a bet.

Primary controls

A – ZCut letters into the inscription — they come off the rack (or out of the mold you are recasting). ⌫ takes the last one back. 1 – 6Target a mold. 1–3 are yours, 4–6 your rival's. Or just click one. ↵ EnterConfirm the armed action — STAMP, RAID, RECAST or POUR. EscClear the inscription.

?Controls, any time. / opens the same panel, and so does Esc when nothing is selected. Every letter key is spoken for by the inscription, so the controls live on ?. –Mute the foundry (the ♪ button does the same). 0Pass.

Paused — Controls

Primary

A – ZCut letters into the inscription. They are drawn from the mold you are recasting first, then from the rack. ⌫ un-picks the last. 1 – 6Target a mold — 1–3 yours, 4–6 the rival's. Press it again to drop the target. Clicking or tapping a mold does the same. ↵ EnterConfirm whatever the action bar has armed. EscClear the inscription and the target. With nothing selected, Esc opens this panel instead.

Secondary

?This panel, any time — pause and controls are one screen. / is the same key without shift and does the same. EscOpens this panel whenever nothing is selected. (With an inscription or a mold in hand it clears that first.) 0Pass your cast. –Mute the foundry. The ♪ button in the header does the same. clickEverything is clickable too — stamps, molds, the action bar. Nothing needs two inputs at once, and the whole game is playable from the keyboard alone.

The four actions

STAMPRack letters only, four or more, a real word. It becomes an unpoured mold on your bench — you may hold three. RAID
RECASTTarget any unpoured mold, yours or theirs. Use every one of its stamps plus at least one from the rack to cut a strictly longer word that does not read the old name inside it. Raiding theirs needs a free slot; recasting your own reuses it. POURTarget one of your molds, confirm with the inscription empty. Cast forever, safe from raids, worth length². PASSDown tools. If both founders pass in a row the tower is declared finished.

The bag holds 88 stamps and the manifest shows the next six — nothing is hidden from either founder. When the bag runs dry the last firing gives each of you four more casts, and the second bench always takes the closing cast — the first bench opens the tower, the second closes it. Four hundred mirror matches say the two benches come out level.
