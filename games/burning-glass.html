Syracuse · 212 BC

BURNING GLASS

The Roman fleet crowds the harbour, already in range of the walls. You command Archimedes' mirror corps: one ray of sunlight leaves the Great Mirror on the tower, and bronze mirror-barges — dragged wherever you want them — fold that ray through the fleet. Every bounce multiplies its heat ×1.4, so a four-bounce chain incinerates an armoured galley a direct beam would only char. The sun rises and sets across ten waves, clouds kill your light without warning, and at dusk the Sambuca comes.
