Alpine dawn · sling-load operations

LONGLINE

Build the refuge before the light goes

Eight loads sit on the valley depot pad and a bare rock shoulder waits 220 m away and 135 m up. You have a small orange utility ship and thirty metres of line. Hover the hook onto a load, lift it, and set it into the marked footprint gently — the whole trick is the pendulum. Fly the machine over the load and the swing dies; wrestle it and the swing eats you. The wind wakes up as you climb toward the ridge. Two spare loads. Three wrecks and the build is over. Lower time wins.
