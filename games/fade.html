The craft: pick your gear at the top — the box will refuse a downshift that over-revs, and once you are too fast in too high a gear you are out of options. Run the jake at stage 3. Use the service brakes in hard snubs (brake to shed 10 mph, then fully release so the drums cool in the airstream) instead of dragging them. Above 250°C they start to fade; at 700°C they burn. Two gravel runaway ramps will save you, but not your time.
