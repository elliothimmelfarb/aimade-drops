An orb-weaver at dusk

The garden is going dark and your web is only an idea. Draw silk between the branches — every thread you spin is a real, sagging, humming rope, and every thread you cross makes a knot the whole web can feel. Flies drift in. Beetles blunder through. Wasps come looking for a fight. Eat, or the night eats you. Three nights until dawn.
