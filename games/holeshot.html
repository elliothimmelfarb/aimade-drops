HOLESHOT

Top Fuel · 1000 feet · four rounds, one winner

Eleven thousand horsepower and nowhere to put it. Heat the slicks in the water box, bump into the beams, cut the light — then find out whether the track takes it or the tyres go up in smoke. The stripe decides the round, and reaction time counts: a slower car can still win.

How a pass works

1 — Burnout. Hold throttle in the water box until the tyre meter reaches the green window, then release. 2 — Stage. Tap throttle to roll into the beams. 3 — Tree. Three ambers together, green 0.4s later; leave early and you are red. 4 — Pass. Watch the wheelstand and detonation meters — breathe the throttle if either climbs. 5 — Shutdown. Chutes, then brakes, before the sand.
