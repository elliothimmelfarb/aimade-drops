TUNDRA

Golden hour on a glacial gravel bar. Three rounds, one taildragger,
and a valley wind that decides how short you can make it.

1Score = takeoff feet + landing feet. Takeoff is measured from brake release to wheels-up. Landing is measured from the chalk line to full stop.
2Never touch down before the line. A wheel even a foot short scratches the round — flat 400 ft penalty.
3Lower is better. Three rounds, added together. Beat the rivals' totals for a medal.

Watch the sock. Wait for the gust — a headwind you take off into is free runway.
