Silo 9 · Decommissioned · Flooding

You are a scrap custodian robot at the bottom of a deep-bore launch silo, and the coolant is coming up. You have no jump — only an over-chambered scrap cannon. Every shot kicks you the other way. The gun is your legs, your weapon and your air-brake.

Fire straight down to go up. Fire sideways to steer. Fire upward to kill your fall. The drum holds 6 shells and snaps back to full the instant you touch any girder or wall — so in the air, shells are altitude. Spend them climbing, or spend them on the drones. Grab floating shells to stretch a long run to 9.
Reach the blast doors at 1000 m. Then keep going, if you're greedy.
