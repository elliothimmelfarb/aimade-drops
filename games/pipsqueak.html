PIPSQUEAK

Sector fighter control, 1940

Raids are coming in over the sea. Radar plots them while they are over water. Observer posts phone in rough positions once they cross the coast. Scramble your three squadrons, vector them ahead of each raid, climb above it, and keep the bombs off the towns.

Your own fighters report a true position only on their quarter of the clock. Between fixes the table dead-reckons from your orders, and the wind is not in the sums.
