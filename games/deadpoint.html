Outside Broadcast · The Hebrides · April 1967

DEADPOINT

The First Ascent of The Clett · live, at teatime

Fifty-five metres of red sandstone standing out of the North Atlantic, four cameras on the swell below, and the entire nation watching you over their tea. Get to the top. Plant the flag.

Your score is the total time of the ascent — and a fall costs you the lot. You swim back to the boulder and start again. Five duckings and the safety boat takes you off.

Paused — Controls

Primary

←↑→↓ / mouse / drag
REACH — move the free hand's target. The hand is pulled there by its own muscles; it will not teleport.
Space / click / GRAB
GRAB & LET GO — press near a hold to latch it. With both hands on, the next press releases the hand that has been on longest, so presses alternate in a climbing rhythm.
A D / ◀ ▶
LEAN — shift your hips. Puts your weight under the support hand (stops a barn-door), unweights a hand, and lets you rest.
W (hold) / DYNO
DYNO — hold to crouch and coil, release to leap toward the cursor. Catch at the top of the arc, where you are weightless: that is the deadpoint. Catch early or late and you are moving too fast to hold on.

The rock. JUGS are buckets — hang all day. CRIMPS are thin edges — strong, but they pump you fast. SLOPERS are rounded — they only hold if you pull straight down, which the wind up top does not want you to do. Watch your forearms: pale is fresh, red is gone. Both gone and your hands open.
