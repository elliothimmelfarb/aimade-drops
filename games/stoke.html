The Down Test Run · 3rd July 1938
STOKE
No. 468 “Merganser” · Streamlined Pacific · 240 tons behind the drawbar
Garter blue, cream lining, six coaches and a dynamometer car. You are driver and fireman on the footplate this evening, and the world steam record — 124.5 mph, held abroad — is not ours.

Away from the platform without tearing the wheels loose. Two miles of 1 in 200 to the summit: get the fire deep and the needle on the red line before you crest it, because everything after that is downhill and there is no time to make steam.

Then let her go. And remember the junction at the bottom — 100 mph on the curve, and a vacuum brake that wants the better part of two miles to think about it.

Arrow keys work as well as WASD. Mouse: drag the big lever on the footplate panel, click FIRE / WATER / the cutoff arrows. Press H or ? any time to see these controls.

The footplate triangle: fire feeds pressure, pressure makes speed, speed burns the fire and the water. The injector is the only way to put water back — and it costs you pressure every second it runs. Watch the big-end bar: past 90 mph the middle bearing heats, and it does not cool quickly.
