HYZER

SIX HOLES · RUBHA MACHAIR LINKS · RIGHT HAND BACKHAND

A disc is a gyroscope. Thrown fast it rolls right and turns; as it slows it rolls left and fades. Nothing here is a scripted curve — the shape comes out of the disc's own pitching moment precessing against its spin. So you never aim at the basket. You aim into a line and let the disc bend home. Lowest total strokes wins.
