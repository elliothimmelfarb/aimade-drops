Kontor
two merchant houses, one sailing era, and every order sealed until the wax cracks
Eight seasons on the Baltic and the North Sea. Every price and every barrel in stock is public; only this season's orders are sealed. Send each cog out with a full hold — buy cheap where a town produces, sell dear where it hungers. Build kontors to tax every barrel your rival moves through that port. But if you both dump the same good into the same town in the same season, the market gluts — the price collapses before either of you sells, and that town stays poisoned for seasons. Richest house at the end of the era takes the era.
