NIGHT STAGE · SS1 KIVIKANGAS · 4.2 KM GRAVEL

One special stage. One car. No spectators, no daylight, no second run. Just you, a 4.2 km gravel road through forest and moor, and a voice in your ear reading the road several seconds before you can see it. Yump is what happens when a crest arrives faster than your nerve does.

Your headlights reach forty metres. The notes reach the finish. When the call says flat, it means flat.
