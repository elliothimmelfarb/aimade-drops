PERIAPSIS

orbital salvage · two-body gravity

You fly a debris tug in low orbit around a banded gas giant. A shattered constellation has filled these lanes with junk, and every fragment is a threat to the four working satellites still up here. Sweep the orbit clean. Bump derelict hulks down into the atmosphere. Everything you catch is recycled into propellant — so the only way to keep flying is to keep hunting.

Burn forward to climb. Burn backward to fall. Nothing up here moves in straight lines.
