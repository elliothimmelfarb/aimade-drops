1917. The enemy redoubt stands over the line and the assault goes in at zero hour. Take a mining party under no-man's-land, chamber a mine beneath the redoubt, and blow it before the clock runs out. Enemy tunnellers are working toward your trench, and both sides find each other the same way: stop digging, hold still, and listen.
