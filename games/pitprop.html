Cut the gob on the lane side, shore it with props as you go, pack the fire and light it. When the props char through the stack hinges over the cut and comes down. Masonry has no tensile strength — a tall one will snap in mid-air, so plan the scatter. Drop her down the painted lane, keep off the sheds, and be outside the ring when she goes.
Playable with keyboard alone or touch. Press H or ? any time to see these controls.
