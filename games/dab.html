OBSERVED TRIALS · LOWEST MARKS WINS
The Strathfinnan Trial · Wester Ross · April 1962
One loop, five observed sections, a 250cc two-stroke and a man in tweed with a clipboard. This is not a race. Every time your boot touches the ground to save yourself you are marked one dab; three or more is 3, footing. Stop for two seconds, roll backwards, or fall off, and it is a five. Ride the liaison trail between sections for free. Lower is better. Nought is perfect.
