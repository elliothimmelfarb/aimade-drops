WATERWOLF

Hold the polder line, 1672. The flood is your army.

An invading army marches on your town from the west. You command no soldiers, only the water. Open sluices from the east canal, cut dikes, and flood the polders in their path.

Depth is everything. Knee-deep water (shown blue) mires a column and bleeds its will to fight until it routs. Flood too deep (dark blue) and their punts will float across. Too shallow (wet brown) and they march through the mud. The doctrine: too deep to march, too shallow to sail.

The enemy keeps to the dry roads, which never flood. Cut the road posts to force the columns into your water. Windmills pump flooded polders dry again. Every field you keep dry pays rent at dawn, and rent is your score. Survive 9 days. Five breaches of the town lose it. Water moves overnight too, so set your line at dawn and wait for it to fill. In hard frost the flood freezes into a road, so break the ice.
