LOGOMACHY

a war of words · the last library, after dark

The ink has come for THE LAST BOOK. It crawls in from the stacks, and it has already swallowed half the alphabet. You have a rack of salvaged tiles and a working knowledge of English. Spell a word and its letters launch as burning glyphs that seek the nearest blot on their own — you never aim. Damage is the sum of the letter values, multiplied by length: 3×1  4×1.5  5×2  6×3  7×4  8×5  9×6. Every blot carries the letters it swallowed; kill it and they fall back into your bag. Your targets are your ammunition.
