The Fernbrook Crier · Composing Room · 1912

A LETTERPRESS PANICOUT OF SORTS

You are the compositor. The copy desk drops a headline; you pick the metal sorts out of the case and set them in the stick before the press finishes warming. Whatever you set is what prints — and the whole town reads it. Circulation is your score and your hide. Run it to nothing and the paper folds.

Metal type is cut in reverse. The faces in the case read backwards. Mind your p's and q's.

Worse: the printer's devil pies the case. Compartments get swapped while the engraved labels stay put. A jumbled compartment is your only warning — after that, trust nothing but the metal.
