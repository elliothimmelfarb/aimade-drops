How a tide is settled

Both crews commit in secret, then the buoys surface together. At each wreck the crew with strictly more divers hauls the top crate into its hold. Equal divers foul the lines — nobody claims and that crate gains +1 value (up to 9). Empty wrecks are skipped. Break one of your own banked crates to add its value in extra divers for a single tide; the value itself is destroyed. Highest hold when the last crate leaves the water wins; ties break on crates held, then on crates unbroken.
