KNEELER

MANX SIDECAR TT · F2 KNEELER · 2.98 KM · POINT TO POINT

Closed roads, grey morning, drystone walls a metre off your elbow. You are driving a three-wheeled racing outfit: two wheels in line and a flat platform — the chair — bolted to the left. It cannot lean. It is a rigid tricycle with all its weight out to the left, and the only thing on board that can move is your passenger.

That passenger is you as well. Seventy kilos of ballast on a rail across the machine:

Left handers throw the load onto the outside — the chair wheel goes light and lifts. A little flying is fast and it looks glorious. Past the balance point the whole outfit goes over. Send the monkey out left, flat on the chair, to hold it down.

Right handers pile everything onto the chair and unload the drive wheel — open the throttle and the back steps out. Send the monkey right, out over the rear tyre, for grip.

Straights: let go of both and the monkey folds into the tuck. Worth real speed.

PRIMARY CONTROLS — FOUR THINGS
W or ↑throttle S or ↓brake AD or ←→steer Q / Emonkey out LEFT onto the chair / out RIGHT over the rear wheel. Let go and they crawl back to the tuck. The traverse takes about seven tenths of a second, so ask early.

The CHAIR meter on the right fills as the chair wheel unloads; the red band is past the point of no return. A sidecar also pulls towards the chair under power and away from it under brakes — that is not a bug, that is a sidecar, and you hold it on the bars. Press H or ? any time to see these controls.
