SIDEPOND

The last lock below the summit, summer of 1905.

The summit pound holds nine lockfuls of water and the springs have failed. Every fill you draw comes out of it. When it is gone, the canal shuts.

Pass boats both ways. A boat up then a boat down costs one lockful; two the same way cost two. The side pond banks the top half of the chamber for the next fill. Fill hard on an empty chamber, but ease the paddles with a boat inside: the gate paddle swamps a low bow, and a fast empty sets her stern on the cill.
