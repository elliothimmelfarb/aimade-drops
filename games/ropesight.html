ROPESIGHT

Ring the third bell of a six-bell tower.

The bell swings full circle and strikes once each swing. A longer pull sends it nearer the balance, so the next blow lands later. A check takes swing out, so it lands sooner. You ring one blow ahead of what you hear.

Land each blow in your slot of the row. Ring rounds, follow the calls, hunt from lead to last place and back, then raise the bell to the balance and set it on the stay.

Space / hold upper screenPull. Hold longer to strike later. X / hold lower screenCheck. Hold to strike sooner. P H M RPause, controls, sound, restart.

The diamond on the ladder shows where your next blow will fall while you can still change it.

CONTROLS

Space J ↑Pull (hold). More pull, later blow. X K ↓Check (hold). Sooner blow. Mouse / touchHold the upper part of the screen to pull, the lower part to check. P / EscPause or resume. MSound on or off. RRestart the touch.

A pull or a check only counts during the first part of each swing. After that the swing is committed and the diamond stops moving.

Steady ringing on this bell: a light check after each handstroke, a longer pull after each backstroke. The gap before every handstroke row is meant to be there.

A blow within 50 ms scores 10, within 110 ms scores 6, within 180 ms scores 3, wider is a fault. Streaks of perfect blows pay extra. Overpull far past the balance and the stay breaks.
