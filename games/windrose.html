WINDROSE

Dawn over the river valley. One balloon, three judge-declared goals, and a landing. Drop a weighted marker at each cross; every metre between marker and centre goes on your card. Lowest total wins.

A balloon has no steering. The sky is stacked in layers, each blowing a different way — surface drainage down the valley, a cross-wind at three hundred, a hard northerly higher still. You choose only your altitude. The wind chooses your course.

PRIMARY CONTROLS — THREE KEYS, THAT IS ALL

Burning takes ten to twenty seconds to become a climb, and a sink takes real heat to arrest — fly ahead of the balloon. Watch the wind stack on the right: it is the whole game. Wires cross the valley twice. Land gently — over 4 m/s down and the basket goes over. Press H or ? any time to see these controls.
