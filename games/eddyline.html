EDDYLINE

CANYON SLALOM · CLASS III–IV · 392 M · 18 GATES

Morning light on grey granite, and four hundred metres of cold green water between you and the finish beam. Eighteen gates hang over the river on wires. Lowest total wins — your time, plus two seconds for every pole you touch, plus fifty for every gate you miss.

Green gates you take heading downstream. Red gates must be taken heading upstream, and they hang over the eddies behind boulders. You cannot paddle up the main current — the tongue runs 4 to 6 m/s and you top out at 3.5.

So you use the river. Behind every boulder the water genuinely runs back upstream. Drive across that shear line with speed and angle: your bow bites the upstream water while your stern is still in the tongue, and the boat whips round on its own. That is an eddy turn, and nothing about it is scripted — it falls out of the water.
