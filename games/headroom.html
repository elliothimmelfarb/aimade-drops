HEADROOM

FPV QUAD RACE · 3 LAPS · 14 GATES · DERELICT POWER STATION

A five-inch race quad, at night, through a dead power station. Low across the apron, up the outside of the cooling tower, over the rim, spiralling down inside the shell, out through a blown-out arch and home through the turbine hall.

THE ONE THING THAT DECIDES YOUR RACE
1
Thrust and steering come out of the same four motors. The flight controller mixes throttle, roll, pitch and yaw into four outputs that physically cannot exceed 100%. Ask for both at once and the mix runs out of room.
2
Airmode gives your steering priority. When the mix saturates, collective throttle is pulled DOWN to protect the rotation you asked for — so the quad still turns, it just stops accelerating. Push further and even the torque gets scaled back and you lose the axis. Yaw goes first: it is ten times weaker than roll.
3
Watch the HEADROOM bar. Green means the mixer has range left. Red means the throttle you are asking for is being eaten by the steering. The fast line is the one where you know when to stop asking for both.
