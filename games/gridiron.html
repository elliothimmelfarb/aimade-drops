WARMTH

GRIDIRON

Ice harvest on a frozen pond, January 1895.

The pond is scored into a grid of cakes and the ice house stands empty. Walk the sheet and break cakes loose with your bar. A cake breaks only where it meets open water, so you work from the channel and the shore inward. The current carries every loose cake to the hoist at the east end and counts it.

The ice is also your floor. You cannot break the cake under your own feet, and every cake you free leaves open water behind it. Harvest your way back toward the bank and keep a path home. Fall through and the cold will drive you off the pond. Rain arrives on the third day.

  • Move WASD / arrows, or tap a cell
  • Break faced cake Space, or tap the cake
  • Jump a gap Shift, or the Jump button
  • Keys P pause · M mute · R restart
