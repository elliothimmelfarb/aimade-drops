BONSPIEL

The shot starts when you let go

Three ends, four stones a side. You throw red, the skip across the ice throws yellow. Set your broom where you want the stone to travel, pick a handle, then hold to charge the weight meter and release. From that instant the stone is out of your hands — all you have left is the broom. Scrub like mad to sweep: sweeping melts the pebble so the stone runs farther and curls less. Under-sweep and it curls wide and dies short. Your sweepers tire, so spend them where it counts.

Mouse / touchMove to set the broom · press & hold for weight · release to throw · then scrub anywhere to sweep
Keyboard←→↑↓ aim · Z/X handle · hold Space for weight · mash ←→ or AD to sweep
