WINGSUIT / ALPINE DAWN

Step off the granite at first light and fly the canyon down. Points come from one place only: the ground. They accrue while you are inside 20 metres of rock or timber — closer and faster pays far more — and a heat multiplier climbs to ×8 while you keep it there.
All of it is provisional. Hit the mountain, or pull too low for the canopy to breathe, and the whole number is forfeited. It banks when your feet touch down under a full parachute.
