How a siege is won
Bring the enemy standard down — smash it, burn it, or collapse what holds it up. Firepots keep burning between turns: every shot either side takes, the fire advances a phase, eating timber and cracking mortar. The bucket brigade costs you your shot but douses the three fires nearest your own banner. After twenty shots each, the greater destruction wins.
