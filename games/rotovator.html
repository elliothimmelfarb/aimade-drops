Your tether spins so its tip is nearly still over the ground at the bottom of each swing. Ground pads lob capsules that cannot reach orbit — catch one there and fling it where the contract wants it.

Momentum is the economy. Catching slow mass drags your orbit down; throwing mass up drags it down again; throwing mass down — deorbit and disposal runs — boosts you. Trash is your engine. Decay into the air and you burn.

Null the PHASE needle, warp to the pass, release on the lit arc. Press H or ? any time to see these controls.
