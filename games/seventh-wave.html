SEVENTHWAVE

Dockside · Crane · Cargo

You run the quayside crane at dusk. The crate on your hook swings like a pendulum — move the trolley, read the swing, and drop crates onto a ship that heaves, rolls and lists under everything you put on it. Stack low, stack centred: weight off to one side tips her over.

WAVES COME IN SETS OF SEVEN. Six are ordinary swell. The seventh is a monster — you get a few seconds of warning while it walks in from the horizon. Fill the manifest, then survive one more seventh with the cargo still aboard, and she sails.

Three crates in the drink and the insurers stop taking your calls. Capsize and it ends faster than that.
