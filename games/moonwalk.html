MOONWALK

FREESTYLE · 90 SECONDS · JUDGES' POINTS

Four and a half tonnes on 66-inch tyres, one floodlit dirt course, and ninety seconds to convince the judges. Ramps, dirt mounds, a row of junk cars and a three-bus stack. Crush, jump, flip, spin. Break the truck and the run ends early — but the points you banked still count.

IN THE AIR, THE THROTTLE IS THE ATTITUDE CONTROL. Those wheels weigh 300 kg each — 600 kg·m² of angular momentum riding in a chassis with 8,600. Open the throttle in flight and spinning them up torques the chassis the other way — throttle = nose up, brake = nose down. That is how a real monster truck backflips, and it is the only way you will land anything flat.

First ten seconds: you are pointed at the big double with the throttle already under your finger. Hold W. Off the lip, keep holding it and watch the nose come up — then tap S to level out before you land. Land on a downslope or on cars and the truck thanks you; land flat off a big one and it does not. The clock starts on your first input.
